# Code of Conduct

Campaign Manager welcomes constructive participation from people interested in campaign technology, deployment, documentation and community knowledge.

## Our standard

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- focus criticism on ideas, documentation, software behaviour and evidence rather than individuals;
- assume good faith while still asking for clear technical evidence;
- respect privacy and confidentiality;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, threats or targeted hostility;
- avoid publishing personal data, campaign records, credentials, identity documents or other sensitive information;
- keep political discussion relevant to the technology, campaign operations or documentation being discussed.

Campaign Manager is political campaign software, so users may hold strongly different political views. This repository is not a venue for partisan attacks, propaganda battles or harassment. Technical and operational discussion should remain focused on the product and the practical problems it is designed to solve.

## Sensitive campaign information

Never post real passwords, API keys, QuickStart keys, database credentials, private server details, supporter/member records, voter evidence, identity documents, internal campaign communications or confidential political information in public Issues, Discussions or pull requests.

If a security problem requires sensitive details, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repository Issues, Discussions, pull requests and other public collaboration spaces associated with this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when behaviour materially violates these standards, exposes sensitive information, creates security risk or repeatedly disrupts constructive collaboration.

Serious concerns should be raised through the official contact route listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the [Campaign Manager website](https://www.campaignmanager.ng).
