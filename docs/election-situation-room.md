# Election Situation Room

Campaign Manager's **Situation Room** is the election-day operations surface for monitoring result submission, polling-unit activity and incidents.

The capability described here was verified in the live Super Admin demo. Final public screenshots will be refreshed after privacy-safe election demo data is populated.

## Operational metrics

The verified Situation Room interface tracks:

- **Total Polling Units**
- **Submitted**
- **Pending**
- **Coverage %**
- **Verified Results**
- **Disputed Results**
- **Active Polling Units**
- **Silent Polling Units**
- **Total Incidents**
- **With Evidence**
- **Without Evidence**

## Analytics and activity panels

Additional verified panels include:

- **Submission Coverage**
- **Incident Evidence**
- **Coverage by State**
- **Operational Activity Timeline**
- latest result submissions
- latest incidents
- recent operational activity

These surfaces are intended to help election operations teams identify reporting gaps, disputed submissions, silent polling units and incidents that still require evidence or follow-up.

## Election result workflow

The Super Admin election area also exposes **Manage Elections**, including election name, party, votes received, total votes, status and actions.

Public screenshots should use only privacy-safe demo election records. Do not publish real active-campaign evidence, private political information or identifiable incident material without a fresh review.

## Why this matters

The Situation Room extends Campaign Manager beyond a campaign contact database. It gives campaign leadership and election operations teams a central view of polling-unit reporting activity, result-verification status and incident evidence during election operations.
