# Campaign Manager Product Overview

This page summarises capabilities verified in the live Campaign Manager demo portal during the 8 September 2026 product audit. It is intentionally limited to functionality visible in the running product.

## One campaign operating platform

Campaign Manager brings political structure, supporter/member records, electoral geography, polling-unit operations, agents, internal communication and election-day monitoring into one campaign system.

The live demo verified national campaign coverage across:

- 6 Regions
- 37 States (including FCT in the displayed national total)
- 109 Senatorial Districts
- 360 Federal Constituencies
- 774 LGAs
- 8,809 Wards
- 176,846 Polling Units

These are the totals displayed by Campaign Manager's current geography dataset.

## Campaign command view

The Super Admin dashboard combines:

- political/geographic coverage metrics;
- Regular Members and Polling Unit Agents;
- Coordinators / Admins;
- Eligible Voters and members Without Voter Card;
- new-member activity;
- election statistics;
- member distribution analytics by geography and demographic dimensions.

This gives campaign leadership one place to see organisation coverage and mobilisation readiness.

## Political structure and mobilisation

Campaign Manager supports hierarchy-aware dashboards and member coverage views that drill from national operations into Regions, States, LGAs, Wards and Polling Units where relevant to the configured campaign scope.

Members can be viewed by Region, State, LGA, Ward and Polling Unit. Aggregate coverage screens show totals without requiring public exposure of individual member records.

## Polling Unit Agent operations

The verified agent workflow includes:

- Self Request;
- Leader Nomination;
- Admin Appointment;
- Super Admin Assignment;
- status tracking for Pending, Approved, Rejected, Suspended and Revoked;
- identity and workflow-stage tracking;
- deployment to a registered Polling Unit or an authorised campaign deployment override.

See [Polling Unit Agent Workflow](polling-unit-agent-workflow.md).

## Election Situation Room

The Situation Room is the core election-day operations surface. The verified interface tracks:

- Total Polling Units;
- Submitted and Pending result coverage;
- Coverage %;
- Verified Results;
- Disputed Results;
- Active Polling Units;
- Silent Polling Units;
- Total Incidents;
- incidents With Evidence and Without Evidence;
- Submission Coverage;
- Incident Evidence;
- Coverage by State;
- Operational Activity Timeline;
- latest result submissions and incidents.

See [Election Situation Room](election-situation-room.md).

## Campaign-specific access control

The live demo exposes 23 campaign-specific roles spanning national, regional, state, senatorial, federal constituency, LGA, ward, polling-unit and member responsibilities. Role/permission management includes dedicated election and SMS permissions as well as administrative CRUD permission groups.

See [Roles and Permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

## Communications

Verified communications surfaces include:

- Internal Communication conversations;
- Email Notifications with delivery totals/status;
- Announcements / Notice Board with audience, priority, status and publish/expiry controls;
- managed SMS permissions and service integration.

Some communication screens are intentionally not showcased publicly until privacy-safe demo content is available.

## Community / Self-Hosted model

Community / Self-Hosted Campaign Manager is free to download and use. No software licence key is required.

Manual geography setup remains free. QuickStart Geography Provisioning, Professional Installation, Priority Support, Managed Deployment, Campaign SMS and custom services are optional paid services.
