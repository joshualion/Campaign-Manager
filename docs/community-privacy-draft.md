# Community registration privacy/licence supplement — draft

Version 2026-10-12. This is proposed release wording requiring owner and qualified legal review, including Nigerian Data Protection Act and applicable existing distribution/licensing requirements. It is not a completed legal opinion and does not replace existing licences before approval.

Community Edition is free to download and self-host. New v1.1.0 installations require registration under the reviewed terms. Free use creates no payment obligation. Installation, hosting, maintenance, training, support and assisted upgrades are optional services.

Website referrals record names, email, chosen version, source, request time and optional promotional preference. Installation registration records administrative contact email, configured domain, random UUID, version, status and acceptance/verification timestamps. A dedicated credential authenticates portal requests over HTTPS and is encrypted locally and hashed at the portal.

No voter records, supporter data, campaign operations, political activity, operator telephone numbers, administrator passwords or application authentication secrets are transmitted by registration.

Security, compatibility, version and essential service notices are separate from newsletters and paid-service promotions. Marketing requires a separate affirmative preference and confirmed email. Refusal of marketing does not prevent installation. Email unsubscribe links and in-app preferences support withdrawal.

Proposed default retention: download contact details are anonymised after 365 days; aggregate request counts remain. Installation records remain while registered, with authenticated deletion available. Detailed event history is pruned after 90 days. Administrative access is restricted and CSV exports neutralise spreadsheet formulas.

Before publication, the owner/legal reviewer must complete controller contact details, lawful basis, rights-request process, processors, international transfers, licence compatibility and retention approval. The final notice must be available on the official portal.
